# Dedup simulation results

Generated by `python sim.py --seeds 20`. Each cell averages 20 random storerooms (4 racks x 5 shelves x 1.8 m, ~300 visible items). One full counting pass, then one full revisit pass.

`d` = pose error on the revisit that tracking did not correct (random horizontal direction). Cells are `double% / missed%` of true items. S3 and S6 (rack tags) do not depend on `d`.
